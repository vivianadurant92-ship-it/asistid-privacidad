AsistID privacy policy

Effective from [effective date]

In short

  • AsistID records when a business’s staff clock in and out. To confirm who is clocking in, it can use a photo of their face, only if the person agrees.
  • Entry photos are deleted automatically after a period each business chooses.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or track anyone outside the app.
  • To ask for your data to be deleted, write to [contact email].

1. Who we are

AsistID is a time and attendance app that businesses use to record their staff’s hours from a tablet or phone at the workplace. It is provided by [name of the person or company responsible for AsistID]. For any question about this policy: [contact email].

2. Two different roles

AsistID handles data about two groups of people, and plays a different role with each:

  • Each business’s employees. The business using AsistID decides what data about its staff is entered and what for. AsistID processes it on that business’s behalf and on its instructions. If you work at a business that uses AsistID, that business is responsible for your data: inside the app you can read its own policy, with its contact details.
  • Owners or admins who create an account. For their account data, AsistID is responsible.

3. What data is collected

From whoever creates the business account

  • Name, email address and business name.
  • The account password. Supabase’s authentication service stores it in a way that nobody, including us, can read it.
  • The business’s settings: schedules, overtime limits, lateness tolerance, vacation scale, photo deletion period and the details the business fills into its policy (contact, data protection authority and a paragraph of its own).
  • The name of each device linked to the business.

About employees, entered by the business

  • Name and, if the business enters them, ID number, department and start date.
  • Work schedule, absences, vacations and leave.
  • Each entry: whether it was a clock-in or a clock-out, the date and time, and how identity was confirmed.
  • If the person accepts face verification: a reference photo, a photo at each entry and the similarity score between the two.
  • Each decision about face verification: whether they accepted, declined or withdrew it, and when.
  • Corrections the admin makes to an entry: what changed, who, when and why.
  • The personal PIN, if the person chooses one. It is stored only on the device, as an irreversible code rather than the PIN itself, and never leaves it.

What we do not collect

AsistID does not use location, has no advertising, shares no data with advertisers and does not track anyone outside the app.

4. What it is used for

  • Recording attendance and calculating hours worked, overtime and late arrivals.
  • Confirming that the person clocking in is who they say they are, by comparing the entry photo with the reference photo. It is not used to identify anyone else or for any other purpose.
  • Showing the business its reports and letting it export them.
  • Keeping the service running and secure.
  • Answering questions and requests about personal data.

5. Face verification is voluntary

Before any photo is taken, the app asks each person whether they agree to their face being used to confirm their identity. Anyone who does not agree still clocks in: without a photo, or with a personal PIN. Consent can be withdrawn at any time from “My data” on the tablet; the reference photo is deleted right away and no more photos are taken.

6. Who processes the data

AsistID uses these providers. Each processes the data on our instructions and not for its own purposes:

  • Supabase: stores the database, the photos and the accounts, on servers in the us-west-2 region.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS Rekognition): compares each entry photo with the reference photo, on servers in the us-east-1 region. We do not ask it to keep faces or build any face database with them, and we have turned off the use of the photos to improve its services.
  • [To confirm once billing is implemented: RevenueCat processes subscription and purchase data, such as the plan purchased, payment dates and the app store purchase identifier. It receives no employee data.]

7. How long it is kept

  • Entry photos: deleted automatically after the period each business chooses, from 7 to 180 days, and 60 days if it does not choose another. On the tablet they are deleted sooner, as soon as they are uploaded to the server.
  • Reference photo: while the person works at the business and has not withdrawn consent. If they withdraw it, it is deleted right away. If the business deactivates them, it is deleted after the same period as entry photos.
  • Entries, schedules and corrections: while the business uses AsistID, because it needs them for its attendance records, or until it asks for them to be deleted.
  • Account data: while the account exists. When deletion is requested, the account and all the business’s data are deleted within [number] days.

8. How we protect it

  • Everything travels encrypted between the app and the servers.
  • Each business can only see its own data.
  • The counter tablet does not store the account password: it identifies itself with its own code, which cannot be used to access anything else.
  • The admin panel requires a PIN, stored as an irreversible code, which locks after several failed attempts and closes by itself if nobody uses it.

9. How to ask to see, correct or delete data

  • If you work at a business that uses AsistID: ask the business first, as it is responsible for your data; its contact details are in the policy you see in the app. To withdraw face verification you do not need to ask anyone: it is in “My data”, on the tablet. If you cannot reach the business, write to us and we will pass your request on.
  • If you have a business account: write to [contact email] from the account’s email address. You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete the account with everything in the business.

We reply within [number] days. You can also file a complaint with the relevant personal data protection authority.

10. Minors

AsistID is designed for people who work. If a business registers someone underage, it is up to that business to have whatever authorizations are needed.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we publish the new version on this page, with its date and version number.
